# Customer Data Classification Policy
Version: 2.1
Owner: Security & Compliance

# Purpose

Defines classification standards for customer data processed by Northstar Solutions systems.

---

# Classification Levels

## Public

Approved for unrestricted sharing.

Examples:

- marketing material
- public documentation

---

## Internal

Non-public operational information.

Examples:

- internal runbooks
- architecture drafts

---

## Confidential

Sensitive customer operational data.

Examples:

- deployment details
- support conversations
- internal customer documentation

---

## Restricted

Highly sensitive regulated data.

Examples:

- financial records
- healthcare-related information
- regulated compliance documents

---

# AI Usage Restrictions

Restricted data may only be processed by:

- approved model providers
- approved deployment regions
- approved retrieval systems

---

# Logging Restrictions

Restricted data must never appear in:

- application logs
- tracing payloads
- debugging telemetry

---

# Retention Standards

Customer data retention must align with:

- customer contracts
- regional regulations
- deletion policies

# Customer Success Review Process
Version: 1.3
Owner: Customer Success

# Purpose

Defines the quarterly customer success review process.

---

# Objectives

Quarterly reviews aim to:

- evaluate platform adoption
- review operational metrics
- identify expansion opportunities
- assess AI performance

---

# Required Metrics

Review metrics include:

- active users
- retrieval accuracy
- hallucination trends
- latency metrics
- support ticket volume
- ingestion growth

---

# AI-Specific Metrics

Customer reviews should include:

- citation accuracy
- retrieval recall
- fallback provider usage
- evaluation benchmark trends

---

# Escalation Indicators

Escalate when:

- adoption drops significantly
- hallucination complaints increase
- support ticket volume spikes
- latency consistently exceeds SLA

---

# Deliverables

Each review produces:

- executive summary
- risk assessment
- action items
- architecture recommendations

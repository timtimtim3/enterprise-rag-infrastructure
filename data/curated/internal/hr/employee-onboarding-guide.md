# Employee Onboarding Guide
Version: 1.6
Owner: People Operations

# Purpose

This guide explains the standard onboarding process for new employees at Northstar Solutions.

It applies to:

- engineers
- AI consultants
- platform specialists
- customer success managers
- operations staff

---

# Onboarding Timeline

## Week 0 — Pre-Start

Before the employee’s first day, People Operations creates:

- Google Workspace account
- Slack account
- HR profile
- equipment shipment request
- initial access request ticket

Engineering hires additionally receive:

- GitHub organization invite
- AWS access request workflow
- development environment setup checklist

---

# Day 1

New hires must complete:

- HR orientation
- security awareness training
- device setup
- Slack onboarding
- company handbook review

Required Slack channels:

- #announcements
- #people-ops
- #engineering
- #customer-delivery
- #security-alerts

---

# First Week

The employee’s manager is responsible for:

- assigning onboarding buddy
- explaining team goals
- reviewing role expectations
- scheduling first 1:1
- introducing active customer projects

Engineering employees must also review:

- API development standards
- logging and tracing policy
- AI governance policy
- access request process

---

# First 30 Days

Expected outcomes:

- understand Northstar delivery model
- complete all compliance training
- contribute to at least one internal project
- understand incident escalation process
- shadow one customer delivery meeting

---

# First 60 Days

Expected outcomes:

- own a small delivery task
- contribute to documentation
- participate in sprint planning
- demonstrate familiarity with internal tools

---

# First 90 Days

Expected outcomes:

- independently deliver assigned work
- understand customer escalation paths
- follow deployment and governance procedures
- complete manager feedback session

---

# Required Training

All employees:

- security training
- data handling training
- AI governance overview
- responsible AI usage

Engineering and AI roles:

- RAG architecture overview
- model provider policy
- production deployment process
- incident response basics

# Remote Work Policy
Version: 2.0
Owner: People Operations

# Purpose

Northstar Solutions is a remote-first company. This policy defines expectations for distributed work, availability, communication, and security.

---

# Standard Work Model

Employees may work remotely unless their role requires customer-site attendance.

Remote work is allowed from approved countries where Northstar has employment or contractor coverage.

---

# Core Collaboration Hours

Default core hours:

- 10:00–15:00 UTC

Employees should be available during core hours for:

- team meetings
- customer delivery calls
- incident coordination
- sprint planning

---

# Async Communication

Employees are expected to use async-first communication.

Preferred tools:

- Slack for short updates
- project boards for task status
- documents for decisions
- recorded demos for complex walkthroughs

---

# Meeting Expectations

Meetings should include:

- clear agenda
- owner
- notes
- action items

Recurring meetings must be reviewed quarterly.

---

# Customer Meetings

Customer-facing employees must:

- join from a quiet environment
- use company-approved video tools
- avoid discussing other customers
- follow confidentiality obligations

---

# Security Requirements

Remote employees must:

- use company-managed devices
- enable full-disk encryption
- use password manager
- enable MFA
- avoid public Wi-Fi without VPN

---

# Restricted Work Locations

Employees may not work from locations where:

- customer data handling is legally restricted
- device security cannot be maintained
- export controls apply
- confidentiality cannot be ensured

---

# Travel While Working

Employees working temporarily from another country must notify People Operations and Security if the stay exceeds 10 business days.

# PTO Policy
Version: 1.4
Owner: People Operations

# Purpose

This policy explains paid time off expectations at Northstar Solutions.

---

# PTO Philosophy

Northstar encourages employees to take regular time off to prevent burnout and maintain sustainable delivery quality.

---

# Request Process

Employees should submit PTO requests through the HR system.

Requests should include:

- start date
- end date
- coverage plan
- active customer impact if any

---

# Approval

PTO is approved by the employee’s manager.

Managers review:

- team coverage
- delivery commitments
- active incidents
- customer launch dates

---

# Notice Period

Recommended notice:

- 2 weeks for PTO under 5 business days
- 4 weeks for PTO over 5 business days

Emergency leave may be requested with shorter notice.

---

# Engineering Coverage

Engineers working on production systems must document:

- open pull requests
- active deployments
- incident responsibilities
- handoff notes

---

# On-Call Restrictions

Employees may not take PTO while assigned primary on-call unless coverage is arranged.

On-call swaps must be documented in the on-call schedule.

---

# Customer Delivery Restrictions

PTO during customer launch windows requires delivery lead approval.

---

# Holidays

Northstar observes regional public holidays based on the employee’s work location.
